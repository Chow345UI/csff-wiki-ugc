Procedure for marking 100% tannin solution
1, Fill a cooking pot with water.
2, Add one piece of bark and boil it twice until the tannin level reaches 50%.
3, Remove the pot from the fire and let it cool down. During cooling, the bark will disappear and the tannin level will increase to 75%.
4, pour the entire pot into the tanning pit.
5, Repeat steps 1-4 five times( five pots total).
6, After that, add five more pieces of bark directly into the tanning pit.
7, Over time, the pit will gradually reach 100% water/ 100% tannin. Only then is the process complete
